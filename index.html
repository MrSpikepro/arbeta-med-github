Discovery of the Leopardus tilcayo

Leopardus tilcayo is a species of small cat found in the Andes mountains of South America. It was discovered after a family in Bolivia found an kitten on the road. Having kept it as a pet for a year discovered that it was feral. Prompting them to surrender it to the Senda Verde wildlife sanctuary.


Only 1 Tilcayo cat has been discovered but scientists believe that there are more of them in the wild. The cat is a small feline with a spotted coat and a long tail. It is believed to be a solitary animal that hunts small mammals and birds. The discovery of the Tilcayo cat has sparked interest in the conservation of this species and its habitat.
